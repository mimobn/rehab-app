# Rehab privacy policy

_Last updated: 15 November 2026_

Rehab is a habit tracker that runs entirely on your device. This page explains what that means for your data.

## What Rehab stores

Your name, avatar, tasks, progress, animals and settings are saved **only on your device**: in the app's local storage on Android and iOS, or in your browser's local storage on the web.

## What Rehab sends

Nothing. Rehab has no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It makes no network requests with your data, and the developer cannot see anything you enter.

## Permissions

- **Vibration (Android):** used for the small tap you feel when you complete a task.
- **Internet (Android):** included by the React Native framework the app is built with. Rehab does not use it to send your data anywhere.

Rehab does not use your location, contacts, camera, microphone or files.

## Deleting your data

Open **Sanctuary → Settings → Reset all data** to erase everything. Uninstalling the app (or clearing the site's data in your browser) also deletes it.

## Children

Rehab collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new date.

## Contact

Questions about this policy can be asked by [opening an issue](https://github.com/mimobn/rehab-app/issues) in Rehab's public repository.
